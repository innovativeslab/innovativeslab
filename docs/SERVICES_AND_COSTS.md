# Third-party services and costs
Prices and free-tier limits change. Confirm on each provider's pricing page before relying on them. No service has been purchased or registered by this project.

| Service | Purpose | Cost note |
|---|---|---|
| Domain registrar | Domain name | Paid, yearly. Required for a real launch. |
| Vercel (Hobby/Pro) | Hosting | Free tier exists but its terms limit commercial use; a business site likely needs a paid plan. Check current terms. |
| Neon or Supabase | PostgreSQL | Free tiers exist with storage and inactivity limits (databases may pause). |
| Resend | Transactional email | Free tier with a monthly sending cap; needs a verified domain. |
| Google Analytics 4 | Analytics (consent-gated) | Free. Optional. A privacy-friendly alternative can be swapped into `Analytics.tsx`. |
| Cal.com / Calendly | Booking (optional) | Free tiers exist; not required, the consultation form works without it. |
| Google Search Console | SEO monitoring | Free. |
